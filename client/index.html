City General Hospital · Intake & triage workflow support

Calm, structured patient intake with transparent triage support

PatientFlow collects intake information, symptoms, history, and vital signs, then suggests a priority level for trained staff to review. Every suggestion is labelled “Suggested Priority – Requires Clinical Review.”

Emergency notice: If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for an online assessment.

Workflow support only. PatientFlow does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

What happens to an intake?

  1. Patient or reception submits a structured intake.
  2. Nurse records vital signs; history is preserved.
  3. Rule engine suggests Critical / Urgent / Moderate / Low / Unclassified with reasons.
  4. A nurse or doctor confirms or overrides (reason required).
  5. Patient joins a priority-ordered queue with token & wait estimate.

Demo thresholds are illustrative and require validation by clinical governance before any real-world use.

Designed for real hospital workflows

Multi-step intake

Patient details, visit information, and consent review — with draft preservation and validation. Clinical staff record symptoms and red-flag screening during triage.

Transparent triage

Every suggestion shows matched rules, risk indicators, score, and rule version. No black boxes, no fake machine-learning claims.

Priority queue

Confirmed priority first, then escalation, then arrival time. Tokens, wait estimates, filters, and emergency escalation included.

Role-based access

Patient, receptionist, nurse, doctor, and administrator views — enforced in the UI and on every API route.

Audit & reports

Immutable audit events, printable intake summaries, daily and workload reports with authorized CSV export.

Safety by default

Secure cookies, rate-limited login, validated uploads, and notifications that never email full medical records.

Roles at a glance

  • Patients register, submit intake, track queue position and reviewed priority.
  • Receptionists register arrivals, manage guest intakes, print tokens.
  • Nurses record vitals, run and confirm triage, escalate emergencies.
  • Doctors review escalations, add clinical notes, manage visit status.
  • Administrators manage staff, departments, rules, audits, and exports.
