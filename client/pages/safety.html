Patient safety

Safety commitments

  • Every automated result is labelled “Suggested Priority – Requires Clinical Review.”
  • No diagnosis, no prescriptions, no treatment plans — ever.
  • Uncertain or incomplete cases are marked Unclassified and sent for human review.
  • Overrides require a mandatory written reason and are audit-logged with staff identity and timestamp.
  • Emergency escalation notifies authorized doctors and nurses and creates an audit event.
  • Abnormal vital-sign flags are informational only and are never presented as a diagnosis.

Report a concern

Contact the hospital safety team via the contact page. For emergencies, call local emergency services — do not use this website.
