Privacy policy

Demonstration policy for a fictional deployment. Adapt with legal counsel before real use.

Data we collect

Account details, intake information (demographics, symptoms, history, vitals), queue metadata, and audit events.

How we use it

To operate intake, triage review, queue management, notifications, and safety auditing. We do not sell data or send medical records by email.

Access control

Role-based access on every API route. Patients see their own records; staff see the minimum necessary for their duties.

Security

Bcrypt password hashing, HttpOnly SameSite cookies, rate-limited login, validated uploads, sanitized inputs, and immutable audit logs.

Your choices

Update profile and privacy preferences after login. Contact support to request correction or deactivation.

Retention

Uploads follow the configured retention window (default 90 days). Ask your administrator for the current schedule.
