Frequently asked questions

Does PatientFlow diagnose me?

No. It organizes intake information and suggests a review priority for staff. It never diagnoses or prescribes.

Who sees my information?

Only authorized staff involved in your visit, under role-based access. Reception sees non-clinical queue data; clinicians see clinical details.

What does “Suggested Priority – Requires Clinical Review” mean?

The system proposes Critical, Urgent, Moderate, Low, or Unclassified using visible rules. A trained nurse or doctor must confirm or change it before it affects care order.

What if my condition worsens while waiting?

Tell front-desk staff immediately or call emergency services. Do not rely on the queue screen.

Can I correct my intake?

Drafts can be edited before submission. After submission, contact reception to amend details.

Is my password safe?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies, and login is rate-limited.
