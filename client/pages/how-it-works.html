How it works

  1. Registration. Patients self-register; reception can register arrivals and emergency guests.
  2. Intake. A three-step form captures demographics, visit details, and consent. Each step validates on both client and server.
  3. Queue entry. On submit, the visit enters the triage queue with a token and arrival timestamp.
  4. Vital signs. A nurse records vitals. Each reading is preserved with staff and time.
  5. Suggestion. The rule engine returns a suggested priority with matched rules and explanation, labelled “Suggested Priority – Requires Clinical Review.”
  6. Confirmation. A nurse or doctor accepts or overrides the suggestion. Overrides require a written reason and are audited.
  7. Care. Doctors review, acknowledge escalations, add notes, and move the visit through assigned → in-consultation → completed.

Incomplete or uncertain cases stay Unclassified and are routed for direct staff review.
