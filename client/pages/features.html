Features

Everything in PatientFlow exists to support safe, auditable front-door workflows — nothing more.

Structured intake

Three validated steps: patient details, visit information, consent and review. Drafts are preserved. Clinical staff record symptoms and red-flag screening during triage.

Vital-signs history

Every reading is timestamped and attributed. Previous values are preserved, BMI is computed, and abnormal flags are shown as information — never as diagnosis.

Transparent triage suggestions

Rule version, matched rules, score, risk indicators, and plain-language explanation on every assessment. Confirmation by a nurse or doctor is mandatory.

Priority queue

Ordered by confirmed priority, escalation, then arrival. Tokens, wait estimates, department filters, pagination, and one-click escalation.

Notifications

In-app alerts for submissions, triage readiness, critical cases, overrides, assignments, and status changes. Email carries identifiers only — never records.

Reports & audit

Printable intake and triage summaries, daily/priority/workload/waiting-time reports, CSV export, and an immutable audit trail.

PatientFlow

Suggested priorities only — requires clinical review.
