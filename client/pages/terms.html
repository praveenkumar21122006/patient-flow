Terms of use

Demonstration terms for a fictional deployment.

  1. PatientFlow provides administrative and workflow support only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
  2. All automated priorities are suggestions requiring review by qualified staff.
  3. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
  4. Users must provide accurate information and keep credentials confidential.
  5. Staff must act within their professional scope and organizational policy.
  6. Misuse, unauthorized access attempts, or interference with audit logging may lead to account suspension.
  7. The demo uses fictional data and illustrative triage thresholds that are not clinically validated.
