About the system

PatientFlow is a demonstration hospital front-door platform: intake collection, vital-sign recording, transparent rule-based priority suggestions, queue management, notifications, reports, and audit. It was built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Node.js/Express, and MongoDB/Mongoose.

What it is not

  • Not a diagnostic device. It never outputs a diagnosis, differential, or treatment plan.
  • Not a prescribing system. Medication lists are history records only.
  • Not certified for clinical use. Demo thresholds require validation by qualified clinical governance staff before any real-world deployment.
  • Not a replacement for professional judgement. Every automated suggestion requires review by trained staff.

Design principles

  • Transparency first: rules, versions, and reasons are visible.
  • Least privilege: each role sees only what it needs.
  • Auditability: sensitive actions are logged immutably.
  • Calm clinical UI: restrained colours, text-plus-icon priorities, keyboard access.
